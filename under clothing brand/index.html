Second atmosphere was built around a single question: what does it feel like to be surrounded by something that moves with you rather than on you? Every piece in this collection sits slightly away from the body — not loose, not oversized, but hovering, as if the garment and the person inside it have reached a quiet agreement about space, about proximity, about the difference between being held and being contained.
